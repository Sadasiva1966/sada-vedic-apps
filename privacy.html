Privacy Policy

This policy covers apps published under Sada Vedic Apps. Last updated: 17 September 2026.

Who we are

Sada Vedic Apps publishes spiritual and practical tools for sincere Vedic practice. Our apps include Sadārogya Sandhyāvandanam, with Vedic Seva currently in development. We do not require account sign-in.

Questions: kaka027rot2@gmail.com

Sadārogya Sandhyāvandanam — current behaviour

An ad-free Sandhyāvandanam companion. Android is free on Google Play. iOS includes a 14-day introductory trial, then an optional yearly subscription through Apple. No user account is required.

What stays on your device

  • App preferences (language, PDF/audio settings, alarm toggles, optional Om-tap / blessing sounds).
  • Optional location or chosen city — used on-device only to compute sunrise/sunset Sandhyā timings. Location is not uploaded to our servers or to Supabase.
  • Optional Sandhyā timing summaries used for shortcuts — on-device only; not uploaded.
  • Optional Janma Tithi details you enter (name, birth date/time/place, calculated tithi) — on-device only; not uploaded.
  • Optional Annual Śrāddha details and Matru/Pitru photos you choose from your gallery — compressed and kept in the app’s private storage on that device; not uploaded.
  • Optional Purohith Diary entries (names, phones, addresses, notes, booking times, reminder settings) — on-device only. Import from Contacts reads a contact you select to fill a diary entry; contacts are not uploaded.
  • Downloaded PDF and audio learning files — cached on your device for offline use. These are learning content, not a personal profile.

What we do not do

  • No required login or account profile.
  • No sale of personal data.
  • No upload of diary, Janma, Śrāddha photos, contacts, or location to Supabase or any other server we operate for personal data storage.
  • We do not receive your card or bank details. Apple processes iOS subscriptions. Optional donations use UPI/PayPal under those providers’ policies.
  • No continuous microphone recording or conversation listening. On iPhone, microphone permission—if shown—relates to audio-session support for playback/alarms, not personal data collection. The app does not store or upload microphone recordings.

Network use (content only)

When you download mantra PDFs or audio for the first time, the app may fetch files from our content hosting (including Supabase Storage). Those requests are for download of learning content. Standard server logs (such as IP address) may exist on the hosting provider. The app does not upload your personal diary, birth, śrāddha, contact, or location data to Supabase.

Permissions (only as needed for features you use)

  • Notifications — reminders you enable
  • Location (optional) — local Sandhyā timings
  • Contacts (optional) — only when you import into Purohith Diary
  • Photos / Photo Library (optional) — only when you add Śrāddha photos
  • Internet — content download and links you open
  • Exact alarms / battery settings on Android where required for reliable reminders

Siri / Assistant shortcuts

Optional. On iPhone, Siri Shortcuts can open Daily Schedule, Local Panchanga, Purohith Diary, or flash-pinned Audio Quick Start. On Android, long-press shortcuts / Assistant can open the same kinds of pages. Speech recognition is handled by Apple or Google. This app does not run a continuous in-app listener. Timing summaries used with shortcuts stay on the device.

Payments

  • iOS subscriptions: Apple In-App Purchase. A short on-device entitlement timestamp may be kept offline for convenience; Apple remains the authority.
  • Optional donations (UPI / PayPal): voluntary; do not unlock app features.
