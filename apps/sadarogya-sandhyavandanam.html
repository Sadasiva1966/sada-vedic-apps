About the app

Sadarogya Sandhyavandanam (also written Sadārogya Sandhyāvandanam) helps sincere learners practice Sandhyāvandanam with clear timings, mantra PDF study, audio guidance, and related helpers — without ads or account sign-in.

Main features

  • Daily Sandhyā schedule Sunrise, noon, and sunset windows based on your place.
  • Mantras & audio Veda-wise learning with PDF reading and audio practice.
  • Local Panchanga Tithi and related details kept simple beside your day.
  • Gentle reminders Optional pre-Sandhyā and Arghyam alerts you can pause anytime.
  • Purohith Diary Optional on-device bookings and notes for seva work.
  • Shortcuts Android long-press shortcuts; iPhone Siri Shortcuts to open key pages.

Availability

  • Android: Free on Google Play.
  • iPhone / iPad: Available via your iOS release channel (TestFlight / App Store). Includes a 14-day introductory trial, then an optional yearly subscription managed by Apple.
