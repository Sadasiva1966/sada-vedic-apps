About the app

Panchanga Darshini (పంచాంగ దర్శిని / पञ्चाङ्ग दर्शिनी / ಪಂಚಾಂಗ ದರ್ಶಿನಿ / பஞ்சாங்க தர்சினி) is an authentic Vedic timekeeping and spiritual companion designed for daily rituals, astrology study, and devotional chanting. It operates 100% on-device without remote tracking, advertisements, or account registration.

  • Personalized Tara Balam Birth star-customized daily auspiciousness calculation (Sampat, Kshema, Sadhaka vs Vipat, Naidhana), 27-star ranking, and 9x9 classical matrix table.

  • Muhurtha Calendar & Graha Placements Interactive month calendar with daily vehicle, travel, and marriage suitability badges plus real-time sidereal 9-Graha placements.
  • 5 Limbs of Panchangam Real-time Drik-Siddhanta calculation of Tithi, Vara, Nakshatra & Pada, Yoga, and Karana with Rahu/Yama/Gulika timings.
  • Navagraha Chanting & Stotras Classical Sanskrit Stotram audio recordings with synchronized syllable lyrics and temple sanctum view for all 9 planets.
  • 108 Japa Mala & Meditation Haptic bead counter, 432Hz Omkar resonance, Pranayama breath-pacer, and customizable Brahma Muhurtha & Sandhya alarms.
