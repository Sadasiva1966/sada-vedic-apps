Overview & Sacred Commitment to Privacy

Panchanga Darshini is published by Sada Vedic Apps (developed by Sadasiva) to provide pure, authentic Vedic timekeeping, traditional Muhurat systems, and personal spiritual sadhana. We believe spiritual practice is deeply personal: no user account or registration is required, and your private devotional activities never leave your phone.

Support & Inquiries: kaka027rot2@gmail.com

What Stays Strictly On Your Device

  • Location Coordinates: When permission is granted, your device's GPS coordinates are used on-device only in real-time by our mathematical astronomy engine to compute precise local sunrise, sunset, planetary hour rulers (Hora), and authentic Vedic Panchanga (Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana, Gowri & Bhaskara cycles). Location is NEVER uploaded, logged, or transmitted to any remote server or third party.
  • Personal Birth Star (Janma Nakshatra): Your selected birth star used to calculate personalized Tara Balam and auspicious timings is stored strictly in your device's local storage and is never uploaded, tracked, or shared.
  • 108 Japa Mala & Sadhana Progress: Bead counters, completed rounds, and daily spiritual milestones are saved exclusively in your device's private local storage.
  • Preferences & Settings: Your language choice (Telugu, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, English), temple dark mode toggle, alert sounds, and custom alarms remain on-device.
  • Audio & Stotram Media: Sacred audio recordings, Omkar resonance sounds, and Stotram verses are bundled offline inside the app or cached locally for your practice.

What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not collect your name, phone number, contacts, or personal profile.
  • We do not track your physical movements or store location history.
  • We do not display any advertisements (100% ad-free experience).
  • We do not use third-party analytics trackers, advertising SDKs, or commercial profiling.
  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal or spiritual data.

Permissions Used & Why

  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Required strictly for on-device astronomical calculation of accurate local sunrise, sunset, and Vedic Muhurats.
  • Notifications & Alarms: Used exclusively to deliver user-configured Sandhya alarms, Rahu Kalam alerts, and festival reminders set by you.
  • Audio / Wake Lock: Allows continuous chanting, meditation bell chimes, and Japa Mala counting without the screen cutting off mid-sadhana.

Subscriptions & Payments

Panchanga Darshini offers a 14-day free trial. If you choose to subscribe to optional premium features, subscriptions and payments are processed directly and securely through official Google Play In-App Billing (on Android) or Apple In-App Purchase (on iOS).

We do not collect, view, or store your credit/debit card details, banking credentials, or UPI PINs.

Children’s Privacy

Panchanga Darshini is intended for general audiences interested in Vedic traditions and meditation. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.
